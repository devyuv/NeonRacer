HOW TO PLAY

Steer: A / D or Left / Right arrows, or the on-screen L / R buttons.

Accelerate: W or Up Arrow (auto-accelerate is also on by default on touch).

Brake / Reverse: S or Down Arrow, or the BRAKE button.

Nitro Boost: SPACE or the NITRO button. Drains the nitro meter, recharges as you drive.

Drift: Hold brake while steering through a turn at speed.

Goal: Complete the required laps as fast as possible, finishing ahead of the AI pack.

Rotate your phone to landscape for the best racing experience.
